Jiang Yu follows a young traveller on foot through Xinjiang in deep winter and lets the landscape, daily life and unplanned encounters lead the story. It is shot in the first person. The narration is written and spoken as the journey unfolds, and the camera stays at the traveller's eye level. The result is a close record of movement, distance and the warmth of the people met along the road.
