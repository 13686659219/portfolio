BRIEF INTRODUCTION / 项目简介

Meeting a place through the people who live there.

《疆遇》 follows a young traveller’s route through Xinjiang, allowing landscape, daily life and unplanned encounters to lead the story. Clara carried the project from the first treatment to the final cut, using image and voice to build an intimate record of movement, distance and human warmth.
